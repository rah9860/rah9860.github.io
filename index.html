Welcome to my portfolio!

As a Narrative Designer, I am passionate about creating immersive gaming experiences that challenge players to make meaningful choices and have a lasting impact on them and the industry.

Games have the power to transport us to new worlds, introduce us to new characters, and help us explore complex themes and emotions. Through my work, I aim to craft narratives that not only entertain, but also resonate with players long after they've put down the controller. Whether it's a heart-wrenching decision or a small moment of joy, I strive to create games that leave a lasting impression.

Thank you for visiting my portfolio, and I hope you enjoy exploring my work!
